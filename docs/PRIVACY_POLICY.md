# Musahih Pro Privacy Policy (Draft)

Effective date: [To be filled]

Musahih Pro is designed with privacy by default.

## What We Process
- Keystroke events are processed in-memory for real-time correction.
- We do **not** store typed sentences.
- We do **not** transmit typed content to remote servers.

## What We Store
- App settings (preferences, correction mode, ignored words).
- License metadata (plan, validation timestamps, device binding hash).
- Non-sensitive operational logs (errors, update checks).

## What We Never Collect
- Password field content.
- Clipboard contents.
- Full keystroke logs.

## Security Controls
- License validation over TLS.
- Device binding hash for anti-abuse.
- No hardcoded secrets in source.

## User Rights
- Export/import settings.
- Delete local settings and logs.
- Disable smart-learning features.
